
About François Gougeon

François started with the CFS in 1979, in Ottawa.  As a "Computer Scientist" and an Engineer, he was responsible for the ARIES system, a million dollar prototype image analysis system. December 1980, François and ARIES where transferred to the Petawawa National Forestry Institute (PNFI), where as a "Physical Scientist" he migrated gradually toward more research endeavours.

In 1984, he was granted a CFS paid educational leave and got a Ph.D. in "Computer Vision". Back at PNFI, he started his work on delineating and recognizing individual tree crowns (ITC) in high spatial resolution (30-100 cm/pixel) aerial images while also working on an autonomous forestry robot.

After the demise of PNFI in 1996, he was offered a transfer to PFC where he continued to develop and apply his ITC techniques in numerous projects in collaboration with provincial agencies and forestry consultant compagnies.

This talk is about the numerous forestry applications of the ITC-Suite.
